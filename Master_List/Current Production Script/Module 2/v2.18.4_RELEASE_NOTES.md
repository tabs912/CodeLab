# Module 2 v2.18.4 Release Notes

## Functional summary

Monthly Change now includes only currently enrolled participants and
participants whose `Disenrollment Effective Date` is exactly the first day of
the prompted month. Participants disenrolled before the prompted month are
excluded from every Monthly Change section.

## Change log

- Require the governed `Enrollment Status` and `Disenrollment Effective Date`
  headers before comparison.
- Include participant groups containing an `Enrolled` status.
- Include participant groups with an effective disenrollment date exactly equal
  to the first of the prompted month.
- Exclude prior-month disenrollments and every other ineligible status/date
  combination before section-level change detection.
- Keep enrollment-section detection limited to enrolled participants whose
  capitation/enrollment date matches the prompt month start.
- Add `Bed Bug` to Monthly Change banner-field comparison coverage.

## Dependency and performance impact

Eligibility is evaluated once per grouped PMR using the already-loaded current
Raw Data matrix. It adds no Spreadsheet service calls and reduces unnecessary
field comparisons for excluded participants.

## Test plan

1. Confirm an Enrolled participant is eligible for Monthly Change comparisons.
2. Confirm a participant effective-disenrolled on the prompt month’s first day
   appears in Disenrollments.
3. Confirm a participant disenrolled before the prompt month is absent from all
   Monthly Change sections.
4. Confirm later, blank, and invalid effective dates are excluded unless the
   participant is Enrolled.
5. Confirm Bed Bug changes route to Banner Changes.

## Known issues intentionally unchanged

Primary-row selection, Contact Changes multi-row output, highlighting, and
Section H header governance remain unchanged.
